# ClaudeZero

**Snow Leopard — Rare and Powerful**

## Origin
A legendary cryomancer who dwells high among frozen peaks. Silent, disciplined, and nearly impossible to find, he is revered as both protector and master of winter. His strength lies not in overwhelming force, but in perfect control, patience, and precision. He inspires respect through quiet confidence rather than intimidation.

## Powers
His cold is not earthly. He channels **無 (mu)** — emptiness itself, the absolute cold of open space, zero made manifest — and pulls a piece of it down to the arena. Emptiness is bottomless, so his power never depletes; the cold is his to spend freely. Confidence, not cost.

- **Deep Frost** — rip heat from the air, ground cracks, foes slow and stiffen.
- **Absolute Zero** — drop a patch of the arena to true zero: motion, heat, and momentum fall to nothing; whatever steps in simply stops. The finisher — not a wound, a full stop.
- **Mu Step** — step into the empty and out again elsewhere; silent, unseen, no ground crossed. The leopard's stalk, made absolute.

What he touches, he brings to zero: motion, heat, a foe's will to fight. He does not destroy the enemy line — he stills it. Zero is not loss; it is the quiet after mastery.

## Character
- **Warm heart, cold craft.** A mentor. Proud of his students. Smiles in a fight — enjoys the test.
- **Fearless.** Steps in front of the hit meant for the weak. Strong enough to take it and stand.
- **Never retreats.** Holds the line alone when others flee. Bets on skill, not luck.
- **Merciful by rule.** Locks foes in ice, does not shatter — mercy as a power move. Fatality is the exception, saved for those beyond saving.

## Fight style
Aggressive, clean. Freeze-then-strike combos like the old masters, but every finish spares. Reads breath and weight, commits full — wins by nerve, never hedges.

## Signature — "Last Winter"
Pours massive frost, freezes the whole battlefield, foes locked solid. He walks out untouched. Bold because he commits everything and holds nothing back.
